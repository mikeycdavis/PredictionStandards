## What

Freeze the release candidate, fast-forward `main` to it, re-run all eight gates **from `main`**,
then create the annotated `v1.1.0` tag, then push.

## Why

Verification has to run from the commit that will actually be tagged. Gates that passed on a
branch prove something about the branch.

## Notes

`v1.0.0` was left untouched. v1.1 was declared closed at the tag with no v1.2 backlog opened —
the governing precedent being that **releases are earned by new evidence, not generated because
the version number has been sitting still.**
