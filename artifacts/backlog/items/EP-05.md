## What

A single authoritative disposition, a declaration of how this pack is invoked, and a written
contract for the document it emits.

## Why

An enforcer parsing this pack's output was reading an undocumented shape and inferring a verdict
the evaluator had already reached. Both are places where a second implementation of the same
judgement would appear, and the second one drifts.

## Notes

Three features, three separate version streams, and keeping them separate is the point: the
standards version, the report envelope version and the adapter `schemaVersion` move on different
cycles, and a move in one says nothing about the others.
