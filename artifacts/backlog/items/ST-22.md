## What

Two determinations: the semantic version, decided from actual compatibility commitments rather than
from the envelope's version number; and the meaning of `project-policy.yml`'s `standardVersion`,
measured from the schema, the history and a run rather than inferred from the name.

## Why

Both are decisions, and a decision recorded after the edit is a justification.

## Notes

The version question was settled empirically, against a worktree at `v1.1.0`, rather than by
argument. Patch was rejected because consumers gained documented surface; major because nothing
documented was removed or narrowed.

The policy field turned out not to be stale but **false**: a run evaluated 52 rules including two
introduced in 1.1.0 while the envelope reported `standardVersion: 1.0.0`. Nothing in the tree reads
the field, which is how it survived a heavily reviewed 1.1.0 cycle that moved three other version
fields. Building the check that would enforce it is deliberately left to its own cycle.
