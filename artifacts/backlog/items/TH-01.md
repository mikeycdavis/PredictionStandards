## What

A standards pack that can decide, mechanically and reproducibly, whether a stated prediction
was made to a defensible standard — and that can be held to its own rules.

## Why

Prediction work fails quietly. A probability with no stated basis, an edge computed against a
vigged line, a confidence label that means nothing, a model that abstained and was scored as
if it had answered: none of these announce themselves. A governance layer only helps if it
refuses to convert absence of evidence into a pass.

## Notes

This theme outlives any particular release. The standards catalog, the verdict vocabulary and
the integrity ratchet are all expressions of it; none of them is the theme itself.
