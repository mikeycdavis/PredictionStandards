## What

`schemas/report.schema.json` at 1.1.0, ADR 0011 defining what that version promises, and the tests
that execute the schema against real command output.

## Why

The version promises that documented keys are present with their documented type and meaning, and
promises nothing about the absence of other keys. Without a schema, "documented" has no definition.

## Notes

The mutation tests are the point: the schema must **reject** a removed documented key and **admit**
an additive optional one — the only mechanical form of "additive is minor". Committed in the red
commit so they were known to prove something against an unmodified tree.

The red was diagnostic rather than merely red: the four conformance tests failed on the version
constant and nothing else, which is positive evidence the schema already described every other
emitted key.

`policy.mjs --json` was deliberately removed from this version family rather than given a common
super-envelope. Its `status` vocabulary is disjoint, and a version naming no contract is worse than
no version. `865a670` recorded the finding before solving it, so the disposition is separable from
the implementation.
