## What

Standards 1–18, 50 rules, the evaluator and CLI, the record schema, ADRs 0001–0007, the
integrity baseline and the test suite — the whole pack in one shippable state.

## Why

Nothing smaller is a standards system. A subset would have had to declare which of its own rules
it was exempt from.

## Notes

Five verdicts (`SUPPORTED`, `SUPPORTED_WITH_EXCEPTIONS`, `INSUFFICIENTLY_SUPPORTED`,
`BLOCKED_BY_INVARIANT`, `NOT_EVALUATED`) over three exit codes. The integrity ratchet ships
**without a `--write` flag** on purpose: the first response to a red build would be to run it.
