## What

Eighteen standards, fifty machine-readable rules, the evaluator, the five-verdict vocabulary,
the CLI, and the integrity ratchet that guards the catalog.

## Why

Everything after this is an amendment to it. The epic had to land whole, because a partial
standards pack cannot be self-applied.

## Notes

Zero third-party dependencies, Node's own test runner, and no install step in CI — all
deliberate, so the pack cannot fail for reasons unrelated to what it asserts.
