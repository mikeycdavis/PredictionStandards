## What

Determine the semantic version from the repository's actual compatibility commitments, resolve what
`project-policy.yml`'s `standardVersion` means, then move every mechanically bound identity to
1.2.0.

## Why

Two of these are decisions and only one is an edit. Doing the edit first would have made the
decisions retrospective justifications of it.
