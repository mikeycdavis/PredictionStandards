## What

Establish that changes to the pack are earned by evidence from real adopters, and build the
machinery that turns adoption findings into dispositions rather than into features.

## Why

The alternative is a version number that moves because time passed. Three deliberately
different adopters were run against the frozen v1.0.0 pack, their findings synthesised, and
every finding given an explicit disposition before any implementation began.

## Notes

The governing rule established here: **replication establishes that a phenomenon is real; it
does not establish that the proposed fix is correct.** Two findings replicated across all three
adoptions and still had to survive a separate argument about the standard itself.
