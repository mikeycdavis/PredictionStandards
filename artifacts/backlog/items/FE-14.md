## What

Produce the decision — an ADR or an equivalent committed artifact — that states how a declared
framework version is resolved, which declarations are legitimate, and what the evaluator does with
each outcome.

## Why

This repository's convention is that a mechanism is designed where its consequences can be argued,
and only then built. The version-resolution question has at least one answer that looks obvious and
is demonstrably wrong, so the decision is the deliverable.

## Notes

The feature is complete when the semantics are decided and falsifiable, not when they are enforced.
