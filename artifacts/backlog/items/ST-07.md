## What

Change the ensemble rule's not-applicable reason to state the observation — *this record states
no ensemble* — rather than a claim about the model behind it.

## Why

The old wording asserted something the pack cannot see. The subject is the record; a record that
mentions no ensemble is evidence about the record, not about the model.

## Notes

Implemented first, deliberately, as a tiny control change: it proved the branch, the gauntlet and
the release mechanics before C2 touched anything structural.
