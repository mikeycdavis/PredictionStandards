## What

The release-defining change. Standard 19 with rules R1–R5, an optional
`subject.falsifiability` schema block, the `falsifiability.declared` structural rule, the
`falsifiability.resolution-not-self-determined` manual-review rule, and version gating so legacy
records resolve to `NOT_EVALUATED`.

## Why

Adoption #3 isolated a real property no existing standard covered: a prediction whose resolution
was already determined by the act that produced it is not a prediction.

## Notes

Three guards matter more than the rule text:

- **R3 forbids requiring independence or externality.** They are the obvious readings and both
  are wrong; without R3 a later maintainer would tighten the rule into them.
- **R5 forbids the pack from claiming it established falsifiability.** The structural rule carries
  `assurance: partial`; the substantive one carries `assurance: none` and is attestable only.
- **Legacy records do not get a free pass.** ADR 0008 settled this before the schema changed:
  accepted-as-old-format is not satisfies-the-new-rule.

S1–S5 from Adoption #3 are permanent fixtures in `test/falsifiability.test.mjs`, including S4b,
which marks the limit of what the rule can decide.
