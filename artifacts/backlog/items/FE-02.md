## What

Adoptions #1–#3 plus the cross-adoption synthesis, each recorded as an artifact under
`artifacts/adoption/`.

## Why

The evidence base for v1.1. Deliberately unlike one another so that a finding recurring across
them says something about the pack rather than about a domain.

## Notes

Each adoption was run against the frozen v1.0.0 pack and recorded before the next began, so a
later adopter could not be tuned by what an earlier one found.
