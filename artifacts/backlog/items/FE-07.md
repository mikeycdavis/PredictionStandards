## What

`scripts/ci.mjs` as the single definition of the checks, run inside a pinned ephemeral container,
with a submission path that verifies the exact commit it pushes.

## Why

The workflow used to restate the stage list, which made it a second definition. Second definitions
drift, and the drift is silent until the two disagree about whether something passed.
