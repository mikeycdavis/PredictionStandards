## What

Defects found by reviewing the release candidate, fixed before the tag and recorded as fixes rather
than folded into the release bookkeeping.

## Why

Follows FE-05's precedent from v1.1. A correctness fix that arrives inside a release commit reads
as tidy-up; classified separately it stays legible as what the review actually caught.
