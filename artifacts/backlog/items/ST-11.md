## What

`renderAssurance` reported a single record's assurance profile as though it described the whole
directory run.

## Why

A genuine reporting defect: an operator scanning a mixed directory would read one record's
assurance as the run's.

## Notes

Classified as a **post-review defect**, not as an eleventh candidate — it was found after the
review closed and was never in the evidence set.

Fixed with a regression fixture containing records with deliberately different assurance
profiles, because a fixture where every record agrees cannot detect this class of bug at all.
