## What

Ship a complete standards pack — catalog, machine-readable rules, evaluator, CLI and integrity
ratchet — that applies its own rules to itself.

## Why

A standards document nobody can run is a preference. The initiative was only finished when the
pack could evaluate prediction records, report a verdict, and fail its own build if its catalog
drifted from its baseline.

## Notes

Closed at v1.0.0. The pack's subject is the **prediction record**, not the model that produced
it — see ADR 0001. That decision constrained everything downstream: the pack can only ever
assess what a record states, which is why so many rules resolve to `NOT_EVALUATED` rather than
guessing.
