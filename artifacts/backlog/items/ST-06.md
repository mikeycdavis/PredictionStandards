## What

Nineteen findings, each carrying evidence, replication status, severity, current behaviour,
desired property, the tempting overbroad fix, counterexamples, assurance limit, disposition and
rationale. Output: ten candidates and sixteen named non-goals.

## Why

To produce a bounded candidate set that implementation could not quietly extend.

## Notes

Frozen at `0083d29` as the decision record before the implementation branch opened. The trap the
review was built to avoid — *R1 and R5 reproduced, therefore implement them* — was real: both
replicated everywhere and both still needed a separate argument that the proposed standard was
correct.
