## What

Adoption by a human-outcome risk model, with its own policy file and record set.

## Why

Deliberately unlike Moneyball: no market, no closing line, no settled resolution source. It
tests the parts of the pack that assume none of those exist.

## Notes

This adopter is where the absence of a market baseline stops being a gap and starts being the
normal case — which is why several market-shaped rules had to be genuinely not-applicable rather
than failing.
