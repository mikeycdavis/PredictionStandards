## What

Add a top-level `status` to `check --json`, folded from the per-record statuses with precedence
NOT_EVALUATED > BLOCKED_BY_INVARIANT > INSUFFICIENTLY_SUPPORTED > SUPPORTED_WITH_EXCEPTIONS >
SUPPORTED. Parse-error records became NOT_EVALUATED rather than statusless.

## Why

Unknown outranks blocked because the pack has never converted absence of evidence into a pass, and
aggregation is exactly where that commitment would quietly be lost.

## Notes

Red-first: the meaning was fixed by tests before anything computed it. The proposal started from
the shipped evaluator rather than from the names of the statuses, which is what surfaced the
transposed ordering.

The changelog had denied a change the envelope actually made; `7a921b6` corrected that, and it is
half of why the envelope earned a minor bump.
