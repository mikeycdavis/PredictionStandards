## What

Adoption by a content-classification detector, which replicated finding N1 and isolated the
property that became Standard 19.

## Why

The third adopter was the one that showed the falsifiability problem was structural rather than
domain-specific.

## Notes

**This adoption produced the single most important result in the programme.** It isolated
*falsifiability at prediction time* — a prediction must concern an outcome whose resolution was
not already determined by the act or state that produced the prediction at `generatedAt`.

Critically, it is **not** independence and **not** externality. A prediction may legitimately
concern something the predictor influences. The five structures it produced (S1–S5) were kept as
permanent regression fixtures rather than cited and discarded, precisely because the
over-broad readings are so easy to slip back into.
