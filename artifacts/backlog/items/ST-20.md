## What

Stop truncating `--json` when stdout is a pipe: set `process.exitCode` and return, rather than
calling `process.exit`, which does not wait for a pipe to drain.

## Why

Over the largest fixture this delivered about 214 KB of a 250 KB document — a well-formed prefix
ending mid-string, and a parse error the consumer had no reason to attribute to the producer.

## Notes

Invisible on a Windows console, which flushes synchronously, and reproduced every time in the Linux
container. It became consequential rather than cosmetic the moment an external enforcer began
spawning this argv.
