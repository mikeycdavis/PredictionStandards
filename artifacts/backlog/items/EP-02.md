## What

Run the frozen v1.0.0 pack against three unrelated adopters, record each adoption verbatim,
synthesise across them, then disposition every finding in a review kept strictly separate from
implementation.

## Why

Findings from one adopter are anecdotes. Findings that recur across a sports-betting model, a
human-outcome risk model and a content-classification detector are properties of the pack.

## Notes

Three adoptions were declared enough and the evidence set was frozen before disposition began.
Stopping was a decision, not an exhaustion — a fourth adopter would have produced more findings
and no more confidence in the ones already replicated.
