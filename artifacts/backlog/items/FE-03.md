## What

A disposition for every adoption finding — accept, reject, defer, classify as policy or default
behaviour, or record as a known framework boundary — with no catalog, detector, schema or
threshold change permitted during the review.

## Why

To keep the question *is this real?* separate from the question *is the proposed fix correct?*

## Notes

Nineteen findings dispositioned; ten accepted as candidates; **sixteen explicitly recorded as
things v1.1 will not attempt to solve.** The non-goal list is the more useful half — it is what
stops the next release rediscovering settled arguments.

Each finding carries the full chain: evidence, replication status, severity, current behaviour,
desired property, the tempting overbroad fix, counterexamples, assurance limit, disposition and
rationale. The "tempting overbroad fix" field earned its place repeatedly.
