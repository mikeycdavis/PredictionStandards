## What

One definition of the checks, run in an ephemeral container, and a submission path that can only
publish a commit that pipeline actually verified.

## Why

A green transcript above a push proves less than it appears to: minutes pass, an editor autosaves,
a rebase lands, and the commit that reaches the remote is one no check ever saw. Nothing about that
sequence looks wrong afterwards, which is why it needs a guard rather than a convention.

Landed on `main` through PR #1 before the interoperability work began, which is why it is a
