## What

A currency tolerance parameter, a corrected overround comparison, and provenance reconciliation
of `dataAsOf` against the record's sources.

## Why

Three findings that replicated across adopters and were pure arithmetic — no policy argument to
have.

## Notes

**C5 over-fired on its first implementation.** It compared `dataAsOf` against the *oldest*
source, which fails a legitimate record that scores a page fetched today against an April
corpus. The correct comparison is against the **newest** source. Fixture S2 caught it, and a
regression test now pins the direction — the kind of error that reads as correct until a real
record disagrees.
