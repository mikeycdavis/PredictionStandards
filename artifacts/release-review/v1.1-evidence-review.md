# v1.1 evidence review

**Status: disposition. This document changes no standard, rule, threshold, detector, schema, or v1.0
artifact, and opens no implementation branch.**

The evidence set is **frozen**: Adoptions [#1](../adoption/01-moneyball.md),
[#2](../adoption/02-burnout.md), and [#3](../adoption/03-deadinternet.md), classified in
[`SYNTHESIS.md`](../adoption/SYNTHESIS.md). Nineteen findings. No further adoption will be run before
v1.1 scope is fixed.

**The rule governing this review:** replication establishes that a phenomenon is *real*. It does not
establish that any particular correction is *right*. Every finding below therefore carries a
**tempting overbroad fix** and its **counterexamples** — the structures a naive correction would
damage — and no finding is accepted until a mechanism survives them. Two findings (R1, R5) reproduced
independently; for both, the surviving correction is **narrower** than the finding first suggested.

## Disposition vocabulary

| Disposition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **ACCEPT** | A v1.1 candidate. The property is established and a mechanism survives its counterexamples. |
| **REJECT** | No change. Either not a defect, or the evidence argues against every proposed correction. |
| **DEFER** | Real, under-evidenced, or the correct correction is genuinely undecided. Each records what would settle it. |
| **POLICY/DEFAULT** | Resolvable without touching rule identity — a default, a schema description, or a document. |
| **BOUNDARY** | A limit of what a declaration-reading evaluator can establish. Recorded permanently, never scheduled. |

# Class 1 — Reproduced across adopters

## R1 — The ensemble not-applicable reason asserts a fact it did not observe

**Evidence.** [`scripts/records.mjs:173`](../../scripts/records.mjs) marks the four `ensemble.*` rules
not-applicable with:

```text
This record names no ensemble; a single method produced the prediction.
```

The first clause is observed. The second is inferred from an absent field and stated as fact. In
Moneyball (#1, F2) it is unsupported; in BurnoutPredictor (#2, G2) it is **contradicted by the
source**, which combines five weighted subscores under a renormalizing average; in
DeadInternetDetector (#3) the same weighted-composite shape recurs.

**Replication status.** Present in all three adopters. Independently reproduced twice.

**Severity / impact.** Medium. No verdict is wrong — the disposition is correct in all three cases.
The damage is to the pack's own `OBSERVED` / `INFERRED` discipline, asserted at the exact moment a
reader is being told why a rule was *not* checked, which is where unearned confidence is cheapest to
sell.

**Current behavior.** Absence of `record.ensemble` ⇒ four rules not-applicable, with the sentence
above. No other applicability reason in the file over-claims: the abstention reason describes the
output type, the market reason quotes `market.reason` verbatim, and the edge and EV reasons state
only "This record states no edge/expected value."

**Desired property.** A not-applicable reason states what was observed about the record and nothing
further. It may say a block is absent. It may not say what produced the prediction instead.

**Tempting overbroad fix.** Make the absence *declarable* — an `ensemble.declaredAbsent` block
mirroring `market`, enforced by a new required rule — so the pack learns the fact instead of guessing
it.

**Counterexamples.** All three adopters compute weighted composites that are **not** ensembles in
Standard 11's sense (multiple methods estimating one quantity), and none would know which way to
declare. A required declaration therefore manufactures exactly the false statement the finding is
about: an honest adopter, offered `singleMethod: true` and a composite pipeline, ticks the box. The
same overreach in its stronger form — making the ensemble rules *fire* on absence — is refuted by the
dispositions being correct in every observed case. See also N2, deferred, which is the general
version of this question.

**Assurance limit.** After the correction the pack still cannot establish whether an ensemble exists.
It stops claiming to know. The corrected sentence must not be read as evidence about methodology, and
the rule's assurance is unchanged.

**Disposition: ACCEPT — candidate C1.**

**Rationale.** The narrowest correction that achieves the property is deleting an unsupported clause
from one string. It touches no rule identity, no applicability, no schema, no threshold, and no
verdict. It is the rare case where the mature fix is smaller than the finding — and three adopters
agree the *disposition* was right all along.

## R5 — A prediction may name its own already-computed output as its resolution source

**Evidence.** #2 record G1 and #3 record S4. S4 maps `Analysis.overallScore` (0–100) to
`output.probability` by dividing by 100 and names `Analysis.overallScore for this analysis` as
`subject.resolutionSource`. Verdict: **`SUPPORTED`, 100%**. The resolution target was computed by the
same run, before `generatedAt`. No later observation can disagree with the prediction, because the
observation *is* the prediction.

**Replication status.** Independently reproduced across two structurally unrelated derived-score
systems — different domain, codebase, and score construction. The strongest evidence in the set.

**Severity / impact.** **Highest of the nineteen.** A record that is incapable of being wrong reaches
the pack's top verdict at full score. This is the false-assurance failure the pack exists to prevent,
occurring inside the pack.

**Current behavior.** `subject.resolutionSource` is a required non-empty string
([`schemas/prediction-record.schema.json:105`](../../schemas/prediction-record.schema.json)). Nothing
constrains what it names or when that source becomes knowable. `subject.resolveBy` exists and is
optional; Standard 13 R5 already forbids conflating it with `expiresAt`, so the vocabulary is present
but unenforced.

**Desired property.** **Falsifiability at prediction time**: the outcome must be undetermined at
`generatedAt`. Adoption #3 established this by elimination against five deliberately chosen
structures — the acceptance test any proposed mechanism must pass:

```text
S1 descriptive index                   → do not misclassify as a probability
S2 calibrated score → future outcome   → permit  (falsifiable via independence: blind panel)
S3 model probability → future index    → permit  (falsifiable via externality: Google's index)
S4 rescaled completed score → itself   → REJECT  (determined before generatedAt)
S5 forecast → future derived score     → permit  (falsifiable via futurity alone)
```

**Tempting overbroad fix.** Require the resolution source to be **independent** of the predictor — no
shared methodology, no shared pipeline, ideally external.

**Counterexamples.** **S5 refutes it outright.** S5 is resolved by *the same analyzer, running the
same methodology on the same upstream data* — maximal non-independence, zero externality — and is a
completely legitimate forecast of what a future re-analysis will report about the page as it exists
then. **S2 refutes the weaker externality form**: its resolver is an internal review panel. And #2's
honest record B resolves against user-entered check-ins, internal to the product and genuinely fine.
So:

| Candidate property | Verdict | Refuted by |
|---|---|---|
| Externality | not required | S5, S2 |
| Independent observation | not required | S5 |
| Semantic correspondence | necessary, not sufficient | S4 has it |
| **Falsifiability at prediction time** | **this is the property** | separates S4 from S2/S3/S5 exactly |

**A second tempting fix, and its limit.** Require `subject.resolveBy`, strictly after `generatedAt`.
It passes the acceptance test as observed — S2/S3/S5 carry future dates, S4 has none — and it is
cheap. It fails on two counts. It is **defeatable**: control record `S4b` adds a meaningless
`resolveBy` and returns to `SUPPORTED` at 100%. And it has a **legitimate counterexample of its own**
— an open-ended prediction ("will this ever be acquired?") has no honest resolution date, so the rule
would reject a valid structure to catch an invalid one. The mechanism this review prefers instead is
a *positive declaration* that the resolution target was undetermined at `generatedAt`, with a basis.
That has no natural-omission case to exploit, states the property directly rather than a correlate of
it, and follows the mechanism the market family already relies on: absence must be **declared with a
reason**, never inferred from silence.

**Assurance limit — stated plainly, because this is the finding most likely to be over-sold.**
Falsifiability is **not machine-checkable** from the artifact. A declaration is a claim, not an
observation. Whatever ships must therefore be `manual-review` and attestable, with `partial` or no
assurance and an `$assuranceNote` saying that the pack checks the declaration was *made*, not that it
is *true* — the same honesty the outcome-bias rules already practise. A v1.1 that presents this as
solved has reproduced R1's error at a much larger scale.

**Disposition: ACCEPT the property — candidate C2. REJECT any independence or externality
requirement. DEFER the `resolveBy` ordering check.**

**Rationale.** The phenomenon is replicated, severe, and central to the pack's purpose. The property
is now precisely stated and has a five-case acceptance test attached, which is more than most
findings ever earn. What is *not* established is that it can be automated — and Adoption #3's most
valuable contribution was ruling out the fix that #2 alone would have suggested. Accepting the
property while refusing to fake its enforcement is the disposition the evidence supports.

**Constraints on any C2 implementation** (binding, derived from the evidence, not from preference):

1. It must pass the five-structure acceptance test. A rule that catches S4 but rejects S2 or S5 has
   not captured the property.
2. It must not require independence, externality, or a third party.
3. Its assurance must be honest; it may not be represented as establishing falsifiability.
4. Any new required field breaks existing v1.0 records. Backward-compatible evolution is a repository
   requirement, so a new `subject` field must be optional-with-a-rule or gated by `schemaVersion` —
   an implementation constraint recorded here so it is not discovered late.
5. If it adds a rule, `EVALUATED_RULES` and `frameworkCoverage` must move truthfully. A rule that is
   `manual-review` is `not-evaluated` until attested, and 46/51 is a better number than a false 47.

## R2 — All three adopters store confidence twice, numeric and categorical

**Evidence.** Moneyball `Confidence DECIMAL(5,4)` + `ConfidenceRating`; BurnoutPredictor
`confidence Float 0..1` + `confidenceLabel`; DeadInternetDetector `confidence Float?` +
`aiConfidence String?`. Three of three, independently.

**Replication status.** Three adopters. The most-replicated observation in the set.

**Severity / impact.** None as a defect. High as *evidence about a rule*.

**Current behavior.** `confidence.not-probability` (forbidden, non-exemptible) rejects a **published
tier** matching `/^[0-9.]+\s*%?$/`. It governs the representation the record publishes. It says
nothing about what the adopter stores internally, and no adopter's dual representation triggered it.

**Desired property.** The question the review must answer, per the reviewer's framing: does the
prohibition govern the *interpretation* of the two quantities, or merely their *coexistence*? It
governs interpretation — correctly. A numeric evidence-quality measure and a categorical publication
tier are different quantities and may legitimately coexist. What must never happen is the numeric one
being published as, or read as, a probability.

**Tempting overbroad fix.** Read three-of-three convergence as evidence that the pack should
*accommodate* the numeric field — a `confidence.score` alongside `confidence.tier`.

**Counterexamples.** In two of the three the numeric field is `0..1`, sitting in the same record as a
probability and visually indistinguishable from it. Admitting it into the schema would place the two
quantities side by side in the published artifact, which is precisely the conflation
`confidence.not-probability` is non-exemptible about. Convergent adopter practice is evidence that
the *risk* is universal, not that the pack should adopt the risky shape.

**Assurance limit.** Unchanged. The pack checks the published tier; it cannot see what the producer
stores.

**Disposition: REJECT as a defect. POLICY/DEFAULT — candidate C10 (documentation).**

**Rationale.** The rule is right and is doing exactly its job. All three adopters already have the
categorical field it requires, so it costs them nothing. What is missing is a sentence in Standard 16
saying that the coexistence is legitimate and naming which representation is safe to publish —
without it, an adopter reading a non-exemptible prohibition may over-comply by deleting a useful
internal measure.

## R3 — No adopter can abstain

**Evidence.** Both #1 and #2 always emit. Moneyball gates *betting* downstream; BurnoutPredictor
degrades *confidence*. Both hold the concept locally (Moneyball's MMA "cannot bet", BurnoutPredictor's
per-dimension `score: null` and "Learning your baseline" state) and lose it at the output.

**Replication status.** Two adopters. **Severity.** Not a defect. **Current behavior.** Standard 17
makes abstention a first-class positive output; #2 demonstrated the full progression
`SUPPORTED` → `BLOCKED_BY_INVARIANT` → `SUPPORTED` abstention, where the last step changed the output
and not the evidence.

**Desired property / tempting fix / counterexamples.** No change is proposed and none is tempting.
The finding is evidence *for* the standard.

**Assurance limit.** N/A. **Disposition: REJECT (no change).** **Rationale.** This is the pack
working. Recorded as the strongest observed argument for Standard 17 and as adoption-guidance
material, not as v1.1 scope.

# Class 2 — Adopter-specific

**On evidentiary status.** A1, A2, and A3 were each exercised by exactly one adopter **because the
other two contain no market, no expected value, and no homogeneous ensemble at all**. That is not a
failed reproduction; it is an untested cell. Their *blast radius* is confined to the money and market
path. Their *validity* is not reduced, and none is downgraded on that basis.

## A1 — `tolerance` conflates probability space and currency space

**Evidence.** #1. An expected value rounded to cents (`6.99`) is compared against the exact recompute
(`6.988`) under `parameters.tolerance`, whose default is `1e-4`. The difference is `0.002`. The
record fails permanently, and rounding to the minor unit is the correct thing for the adopter to do.

**Replication status.** One adopter of one that has an expected value. Untested elsewhere.

**Severity / impact.** Medium-high within scope. It makes `ev.recomputable` unusable for any record
that publishes money at real-world precision — which is every money-bearing record.

**Current behavior.** One `tolerance` parameter serves edge recomputation, EV recomputation, and
de-vigging. Its schema description names all three
([`schemas/project-policy.schema.json:88`](../../schemas/project-policy.schema.json)), so the
conflation is documented but not resolved.

**Desired property.** A tolerance comparison must be dimensionally correct. A currency amount is
compared at the resolution of its minor unit; a probability is compared at `1e-4`.

**Tempting overbroad fix.** Raise the global `tolerance` until cent-rounded EVs pass.

**Counterexamples.** `edge.recomputable` at `1e-4` is exactly the check that makes a fabricated edge
detectable, and `edge.not-fabricated` is non-exemptible. Loosening one number to fix a units bug
would weaken a non-exemptible rule's evidence — the shape the standards-integrity invariant exists to
forbid, arrived at by accident rather than intent.

**Assurance limit.** A dimensionally correct tolerance still establishes only that the declared EV
matches the declared `stake`, `payout`, and `probability`. A misdeclared stake remains invisible.

**Disposition: ACCEPT — candidate C3.**

**Rationale.** A genuine unit error, demonstrated with a worked instance, with a correction that is
narrow (a separate currency-space tolerance applied only to `ev.recomputable`) and that leaves every
probability-space check untouched. The reviewer's rule applies: not reproducing because the
functionality does not exist elsewhere is not evidence against it.

## A2 — A stated `market.vig.overround` is never verified against the quoted prices

**Evidence.** #1. `scripts/records.mjs` D14 recomputes `Σ(1/decimal) − 1` and uses it to decide
whether removal was *required*, but never compares it with the record's own stated
`market.vig.overround`. A record may state any overround it likes.

**Replication status.** One adopter of one that has a market. Untested elsewhere.

**Severity / impact.** Low-medium. It is a published number carrying no assurance, in the family of
rules whose whole value is recomputation.

**Current behavior.** As above. The rule's `$assuranceNote` says the overround "is recomputed from the
supplied prices, so its presence is established exactly" — true of the *computation*, and readable as
a claim about the *stated field*, which is unverified.

**Desired property.** Every declared quantity the record supplies enough information to recompute is
recomputed. This is the pack's existing discipline for edge, EV, interval coherence, and ensemble
spread; overround is the one omission.

**Tempting overbroad fix.** Extend it to verify that the *de-vigged* implied probabilities match the
raw prices under the named method for all methods.

**Counterexamples.** `power` and `shin` take parameters the record does not carry — the reason
`market.implied-probability-consistent` is already `partial` with that limitation disclosed. A general
version would either re-introduce the false-assurance problem or require a schema change to carry
method parameters, neither justified by one observation.

**Assurance limit.** Recomputing the stated overround establishes internal consistency with the
quoted prices. It does not establish that the quoted prices are the prices that existed.

**Disposition: ACCEPT — candidate C4.**

**Rationale.** The computation is already performed; only the comparison is missing. It is the
cheapest available increase in real assurance in the whole candidate set, and it closes a gap between
what the `$assuranceNote` can be read as claiming and what the detector does.

## A3 — The disagreement statistic differs from the adopter's

**Evidence.** #1. Moneyball's model-agreement gate uses **std dev < 0.08**; the pack uses **max−min
spread > 0.10**. For two models these differ by a factor of two, so transcribing the number loosens
the gate by roughly 60%.

**Replication status.** One adopter of one with a homogeneous ensemble.

**Severity / impact.** Medium, and silent — which is the worst combination. An adopter who moves their
own threshold into `parameters.disagreementThreshold` weakens their gate and receives no signal.

**Current behavior.** `parameters.disagreementThreshold` is documented as a threshold. The *statistic*
it applies to is fixed in the detector and named nowhere the adopter reads.

**Desired property.** A parameter states the statistic it thresholds, so a value cannot be transcribed
between incompatible measures.

**Tempting overbroad fix.** Make the statistic configurable — accept `spread` or `stddev`.

**Counterexamples.** The pack's own Standard 10 reasoning selects max−min deliberately: it is the
statistic that cannot hide a two-way split behind a small dispersion, which is the failure the
standard is about. Making it configurable lets an adopter select the statistic that hides their split,
converting a documentation gap into a sanctioned weakening.

**Assurance limit.** Documentation cannot prevent a determined transcription error; it removes the
*silent* one.

**Disposition: POLICY/DEFAULT — folded into candidate C6 (see P2).**

**Rationale.** The defect is that the schema description is silent, not that the statistic is wrong.
A1's counterexample applies in miniature: the tempting fix trades a real guarantee for flexibility
nobody asked for.

# Class 3 — Newly exposed, single observation

## N2 — Market absence must be declared; edge and EV absence may be silent

**Evidence.** #2. `market.reference-present-or-declared-absent` requires a positive declaration with a
reason. `edge` and `expectedValue` may simply be omitted, and their rules go not-applicable quoting
"This record states no edge." Same class of concept, two standards of evidence.

**Replication status.** One observation. **Severity.** Low.

**Current behavior.** As above, and deliberate in origin: a market either exists or does not, whereas
an edge is a derived quantity a record may reasonably decline to compute.

**Desired property.** Undetermined — which is why this is deferred. The asymmetry may be correct.

**Tempting overbroad fix.** Impose symmetry by requiring declared-absence for edge and EV.

**Counterexamples.** Every non-market adopter (#2, #3) would gain two mandatory declarations of
absence for quantities their domain has no concept of. R1's counterexample recurs: a required
declaration about an inapplicable concept manufactures false statements.

**Assurance limit.** N/A. **Disposition: DEFER.**

**Rationale.** Genuine asymmetry, unclear direction, low severity, and it interacts with C1 — if C1's
counterexample is right that declarations about inapplicable concepts are harmful, the resolution may
be to *relax* market rather than tighten edge. **What would settle it:** an adopter that computes an
edge in some records and legitimately omits it in others, or an adopter that omits `market` where a
market genuinely exists.

## N3 — No vocabulary for disagreement among heterogeneous components of a composite

**Evidence.** #2. Standard 10 covers multiple models estimating *one* quantity. BurnoutPredictor
averages sleep-90 with mood-20 to a middling composite: the same hiding-a-split failure, with no rule
reaching it. **Partially confirmed by #3** — DeadInternetDetector's five weighted category scores are
the same shape — but #3 did not exercise it, so it is not promoted.

**Replication status.** One observation plus one structural echo. **Severity.** Medium; a gap, not a
defect — the pack does not claim to cover it.

**Current behavior.** Silent. No rule applies, and nothing false is asserted.

**Desired property.** Possibly: a composite index whose components disagree materially must declare
the disagreement, as an ensemble must.

**Tempting overbroad fix.** Extend the `ensemble.*` rules to composites.

**Counterexamples.** Composite components measure **different things** — sleep and mood are not two
estimates of one quantity — so `spread = max − min` is not a disagreement statistic there at all; it
is a statement about dispersion between incommensurable scales. Reusing the ensemble vocabulary would
produce confident nonsense, and it is precisely the misapplication A3 warns about.

**Assurance limit.** N/A yet. **Disposition: DEFER.**

**Rationale.** The most likely candidate for a genuinely *new* standard in some later version, and the
one with the least evidence about what it should say. **What would settle it:** an adopter whose
composite splits materially and who has a defensible position on what should be declared.

## N4 — The abstention statement conflates two different refusals

**Evidence.** #3, record S1. A descriptive index with **complete** evidence, all five categories
scored, and no prediction being attempted. The nearest available shape is an abstention, which reaches
`SUPPORTED` while asserting `NO PREDICTION / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`. The first clause is true; the
second is false. A legitimate adopter is forced to misstate which refusal applies.

**Replication status.** One observation. **Severity.** Medium — it forces a false statement, which is
a category the pack normally treats seriously.

**Current behavior.** `output.abstention.statement` is `const` in the schema, quoted verbatim in
Standard 17, and **fidelity-checked against source #1**, which fixes the string exactly.

**Desired property.** An artifact that declines to make a claim of this kind should not have to assert
a reason it does not have.

**Tempting overbroad fix.** Change the statement, or make it a template.

**Counterexamples — hard ones.** The string is **verbatim from the source spec** and machine-checked
against it. Editing it breaks fidelity with source #1 and is exactly the shape the standards-integrity
invariant forbids: reinterpreting a requirement because it is inconvenient. Note carefully that the
source says outputs "must include the possibility" of that statement — a **floor**, not a ceiling — so
adding a *third* output type does not conflict with it, while altering the statement does.

**A second, live possibility.** S1 may simply be **out of scope**: a descriptive index is not a
prediction, and the honest answer may be that no prediction record should be filed for it. That is not
obviously wrong, and B2 shows the pack already accepts artifacts that are not predictions.

**Assurance limit.** N/A. **Disposition: DEFER.**

**Rationale.** Real, and correctly identified: the two refusals are not logically equivalent. But the
review cannot choose between "add a no-claim output type" and "descriptive artifacts are out of
scope" on one observation, and the wrong choice adds a permanent output type to the schema.
**What would settle it:** a second adopter producing a descriptive artifact alongside genuine
predictions, and a position on whether such artifacts belong in a records directory at all.
**Binding constraint on any future fix:** the fixed statement is never altered.

## N5 — Method limitations have nowhere to go

**Evidence.** #3. `Analysis.limitations String[]` — the product records what its own *method*
structurally cannot establish. The pack has `data.completeness.knownGaps`, which is a gap in the
*data*. Semantically different; an adopter must fold one into the other or drop it.

**Replication status.** One observation. **Severity.** Low.
**Current behavior.** No field. **Desired property.** Possibly an optional additive field.
**Tempting overbroad fix.** Add it and require it. **Counterexamples.** Requiring it converts a
thoughtful adopter's voluntary practice into a box every adopter fills with boilerplate, and the pack
has no way to check the content — the field would carry the appearance of assurance and none of the
substance. **Assurance limit.** Whatever is written there is unverifiable prose.

**Disposition: DEFER.** **Rationale.** Cheap, additive, backward-compatible, and unevidenced. It is
the sort of change that is easy to make and hard to justify. **What would settle it:** a second
adopter maintaining an equivalent field, or a rule that could actually use the contents.

# Class 4 — Framework boundary

**These are permanent limits, recorded so they are not rediscovered as bugs.** Treating any of them as
schedulable work recreates the false-assurance problem the pack exists to prevent.

## B1 — Under-declaration

**Evidence.** #1, with a working adversarial instance: a producer that sets its own freshness window,
empties `criticalMissing`, and omits an ensemble reaches 100% `SUPPORTED`. *The producer sets the
window that judges the producer.*

**Replication status.** One constructed instance; the property is structural, not empirical.
**Severity.** Definitional. **Current behavior.** The evaluator reads declarations. It cannot observe
facts absent from the artifact. **Desired property.** None achievable — this is what internal
validation *is*.

**Tempting overbroad fix.** Represent provenance reconciliation, independent evidence generation, or a
disclosure standard as *closing* B1.

**Counterexamples.** An empty `criticalMissing` and an omitted `ensemble` have **no independent
referent inside the artifact**. Nothing the evaluator can read contradicts them. Three adoptions have
not changed this and no number of adoptions will.

**The one narrow sliver that is real.** The freshness-window evasion leaves a contradiction between
two fields *already in the record*: `data.freshness.dataAsOf` versus `provenance.dataSources[].asOf`
and `retrievedAt`. Detecting that contradiction is an ordinary in-artifact consistency check of the
same kind as edge and EV recomputation, and it closes one specific evasion.

**Assurance limit — binding.** It closes **one evasion**, not B1. It must never be described, in code,
docs, `$assuranceNote`, or release notes, as addressing disclosure completeness.

**Disposition: BOUNDARY (permanent). The narrow reconciliation check: ACCEPT — candidate C5, scope-bounded by the constraint above.**

## B2 — Mis-typing

**Evidence.** #2, record A. Every field present, internally consistent, completely declared — and the
artifact is not a prediction. Nothing was withheld.

**Severity.** Definitional. **Current behavior.** The pack validates the support for a claimed
quantity; it does not adjudicate whether the quantity is the kind of thing claimed.
**Desired property.** None achievable in general. **Tempting overbroad fix.** Assume B1's mitigations
help. **Counterexamples.** They do not touch B2: record A withheld nothing, so no completeness
mechanism reaches it. B1 is a *completeness* failure, B2 a *validity* failure, and conflating them is
the most likely way to build a v1.1 mechanism that solves neither.
**Assurance limit.** Total. **Disposition: BOUNDARY (permanent).**

**Relationship to R5.** R5 is the one *sliver* of B2 that turned out to be reachable, because
"determined before `generatedAt`" is a fact about the record's own timestamps rather than about the
world. That sliver being reachable is not evidence that B2 is.

## B3 — 46/50 coverage

**Evidence.** v1.0.0, reconfirmed by all three adoptions, none of which found it obstructive.
**Current behavior.** Three outcome-bias/lookahead-evaluation rules have no artifact to inspect;
`integrity.no-manipulation` is partly automated. All four report `not-evaluated`, truthfully.
**Desired property.** Already held: `skipped ≠ passed`. **Tempting overbroad fix.** Add detectors to
reach 50/50. **Counterexamples.** Every one of the four would have to infer a fact the artifact does
not contain — R1's error, promoted to policy. **Assurance limit.** Stated by the number itself.

**Disposition: BOUNDARY (permanent).** **Rationale.** The number is a principle, not a statistic. A
truthful `not-evaluated` is better than fake automated assurance, and if C2 adds a manual-review rule
the honest denominator grows.

## What v1.1 will not attempt

Written before implementation opens, so that scope creep has something to violate.

1. **Independence or externality requirements for resolution.** Refuted by S5 and S2. Falsifiability
   is the property; independence is one sufficient route to it, not the requirement.
2. **Automated verification of falsifiability.** Not machine-checkable from the artifact. C2 checks
   that a declaration was made, never that it is true, and must say so.
3. **Requiring `subject.resolveBy` after `generatedAt` as the mechanism for R5.** Defeated by control
   record S4b, and it rejects legitimate open-ended predictions. Deferred, not adopted.
4. **Making the `ensemble.*` rules fire on an absent block, or requiring an ensemble declaration.**
   The disposition is correct in all three adopters; only the sentence is wrong.
5. **Closing B1.** Under-declaration is a boundary of internal validation. C5 closes one evasion using
   facts already in the artifact and must never be represented as more.
6. **Closing B2.** Mis-typing is not reachable by completeness mechanisms. R5's sliver is reachable
   only because it is a fact about the record's own timestamps.
7. **Reaching 50/50 coverage.** A truthful `not-evaluated` beats fake automated assurance. If C2 adds
   a manual-review rule, the denominator grows and the ratio should get *worse*, honestly.
8. **Changing the abstention statement** `NO PREDICTION / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`. It is verbatim from
   source #1 and fidelity-checked. N4 is real; the statement is not the place to fix it.
9. **A no-claim or descriptive output type (N4).** Deferred — one observation, and whether such
   artifacts belong in a records directory at all is unresolved.
10. **Composite-index disagreement vocabulary (N3).** Deferred — the ensemble vocabulary does not
    transfer to incommensurable components.
11. **Declaration symmetry for edge and expected value (N2).** Deferred — the correct direction is
    undetermined and may be to relax `market` rather than tighten the others.
12. **A method-limitations field (N5).** Deferred — additive, cheap, and unevidenced.
13. **A configurable disagreement statistic.** Would sanction selecting the measure that hides a split.
14. **Lowering `minSampleSize` to 28**, or any other adopter's domain constant becoming a default.
15. **A standard governing decision rules.** Out of the source's authority; C8 states the boundary
    instead.
16. **Adopter-vocabulary changes to the example confidence tiers (R4).** Convergence is confirmation,
    not scope.

## Method note

Three of the nineteen dispositions changed direction during this review because a counterexample
survived: R5's independence requirement (S5), N2's symmetry (the non-market adopters), and N3's reuse
of the ensemble vocabulary (incommensurable components). Two more were narrowed rather than reversed
(R1, C5). The counterexample column earned its place; without it this review would have shipped an
independence rule that rejects two structures Adoption #3 proved legitimate.

**Replication established that R1 and R5 are real. It did not establish what to do about either.**
For R1 the answer turned out to be one clause; for R5 it is a standard the pack cannot automate and
must not pretend to.
